### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Cloud Architect Game Show

&emsp;**Date & Time:** June 20, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Main Content & Activities:**  
* **AWS Architecture Quiz Competition:** Followed a head-to-head game show with 8 teams, 5 members per team and 4 quarterfinal matches.
* **Question Structure:** Each match used 10 questions, with harder AWS and solution architecture questions appearing in later rounds.
* **Knockout Format:** Winning teams advanced from quarterfinals to semifinals; the final was postponed because the event time was limited.
* **Special Skills:** Observed how teams used Minimum Risk and Hope Star to manage scoring risk during uncertain or high-confidence answers.
* **Memorable Team:** Team Ngu Dai Hiep reached the final and showed strong teamwork under pressure.

&emsp;**Outcomes & Values Gained:**  
* Reviewed AWS fundamentals, advanced services and certification-style architecture reasoning in a competitive format.
* Learned to read scenario constraints carefully, compare service trade-offs and communicate faster in team-based decision making.
